I like turning complicated things into maps people can read.

For the last eight years I've designed and built software for researchers, city planners and nonprofits. Most of that work starts the same way: a spreadsheet nobody fully understands, and a team that needs to make a decision from it.

I work across the whole stretch, from interviews and sketches to shipping the code. Right now I'm independent, taking on two or three projects a year, and writing about interface design on the side.

Away from the screen I hike, draw terrible watercolours, and collect old survey maps.
